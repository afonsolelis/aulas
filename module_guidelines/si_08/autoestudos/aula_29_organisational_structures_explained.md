## Contexto e Objetivo

This video introduces key reflections on how power structures and hierarchies influence decision-making and group effectiveness. It invites us to think about the visible and invisible dynamics of authority, how leaders can adapt to or reshape hierarchies, and the strategic impacts that arise from these relationships.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Liderança
**Assuntos:** Liderança e relações de poder
**Duração recomendada:** 0 min
