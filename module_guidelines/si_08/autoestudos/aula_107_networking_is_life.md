## Contexto e Objetivo

"I’m too lazy for networking!" Ever heard this phrase? It’s pretty common, especially among people who are more introverted or shy and think that “networking” just means small talk. However, many extraordinary people in this area would actually be considered introverts — like Věra Jourová, who in 2019 was named one of the 100 most influential people in the world. You’ve probably never heard of her, yet she was the one who pushed through and secured approval for the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe, which became a model for most countries and for our LGPD. Today, as the European Commissioner for Justice, Consumers, and Gender Equality, Jourová is a strong voice calling for the regulation of Big Tech around the world.
