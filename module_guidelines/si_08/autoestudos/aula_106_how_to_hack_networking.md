## Contexto e Objetivo

In this video, David Burkus outlines three rules for building useful relationship networks not only for work but also for life. These rules apply to anyone, even the most introverted. Watch this video as an introduction to the topic of the meeting and keep the three rules in mind.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Liderança
**Assuntos:** Tipos de comunicação e intencionalidade na comunicação
**Duração recomendada:** 0 min
