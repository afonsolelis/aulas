## Contexto e Objetivo

This whitepaper offers practical guidance for designing dashboards that effectively communicate insights and engage users. It emphasizes three key strategies:
 1. Understand Your Audience: Design dashboards based on user needs and context rather than just displaying data.
 2. Simplify Design: Remove unnecessary elements, focus on clarity, and highlight key insights.
 3. Iterate and Improve: Continuously refine dashboards using user feedback to enhance usability and effectiveness.

Why it matters for the Module:
 * Supports UX in Big Data Projects: Teaching students to design dashboards that are not only informative but also persuasive aligns directly with Module goals.
 * Enhances Data Storytelling: Shows how design choices impact how insights are perceived and acted upon.
