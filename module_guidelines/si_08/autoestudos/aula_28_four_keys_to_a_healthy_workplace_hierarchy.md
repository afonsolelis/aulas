## Contexto e Objetivo

Can workplace hierarchies actually be healthy and beneficial? According to the article, the answer is yes—when they are well structured. The author points to four key elements of an effective hierarchy: clarity, fairness, giving people a voice, and valuing everyone’s well-being. The text highlights how each of these elements helps create a fair and productive work environment, and it offers ideas on how organizations can put them into practice to improve teamwork and employee satisfaction.
