## Contexto e Objetivo

How can we apply Pink’s and Sinek’s motivation theories to establish shared goals within a team? In what ways do the differences between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation impact collaboration and the achievement of collective goals? What strategies can be used to prevent and mitigate conflicts, fostering a cohesive and productive work environment?
In this self-study, we will dive into practical approaches inspired by the theories of Daniel Pink and Simon Sinek for establishing shared goals and promoting a collective sense of purpose within teams. We will explore the importance of intrinsic motivation, as presented in Drive, and the power of the “why”, highlighted in Start with Why, to cultivate a work environment where collaboration and effectiveness not only coexist but truly flourish.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Liderança
**Assuntos:** Liderança e mudança organizacional
