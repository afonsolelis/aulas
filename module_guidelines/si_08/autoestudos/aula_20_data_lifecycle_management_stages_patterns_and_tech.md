## Contexto e Objetivo

Data lifecycle management (DLM) is the process of managing your data, from creation and collection to storage and transformation, consumption, and final deletion or archival. DLM aims to ensure that data is accurate, complete, and easily accessible to its users. Steps are taken at every stage to protect data from unauthorized access, corruption, or loss.
