## Contexto e Objetivo

Watch the video: "StatQuest: Principal Component Analysis (PCA), Step by Step," by StatQuest with Josh Starmer. Principal Component Analysis is one of the most useful data analysis and machine learning methods available. It can be used to identify patterns in highly complex datasets and can tell you which variables in your data are most important. Ultimately, it can tell you how accurate your new understanding of the data truly is.
