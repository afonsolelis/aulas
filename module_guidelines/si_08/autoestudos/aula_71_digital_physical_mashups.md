## Contexto e Objetivo

The Harvard Business Review article, “Digital-Physical Mashups”, addresses the growing phenomenon of combining the digital and physical worlds to create new products and services. The authors discuss how companies are leveraging technologies such as sensors, IoT, and data analytics to integrate the digital and physical experience, creating innovative and personalized solutions.

**Assuntos:** Sustentabilidade e ESG
