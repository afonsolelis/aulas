## Contexto e Objetivo

The Harvard Business Review article, “Your Competitors Aren’t Always Who You Think They Are,” discusses the importance of continually reassessing competitors in the market. The authors argue that it is essential not only to focus on traditional competitors but also to consider emerging and indirect competitors.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Negócios
**Assuntos:** Sustentabilidade e ESG
**Duração recomendada:** 0 min
