## Contexto e Objetivo

How can we create an environment where people naturally offer their best? This is the challenge that every team leader has to face. In this video, Simon Sinek describes his perception of the need to build trusting teams, providing security and support for its members.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Liderança
**Assuntos:** Uso de ferramentas de colaboração
,Priorização de tarefas a partir do design
**Duração recomendada:** 0 min
