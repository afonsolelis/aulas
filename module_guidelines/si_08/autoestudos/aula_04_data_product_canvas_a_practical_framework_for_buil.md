## Contexto e Objetivo

The article presents the Data Product Canvas, a practical framework for developing data products, focusing on clearly defining the problem and creating a strategic plan. It is divided into 10 blocks that cover everything from problem identification to risk and impact assessment, aiming to align technical and business areas to ensure the product's effectiveness. The methodology emphasizes that the success of a data product depends more on understanding the problem than on the technology used.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Computação
**Assuntos:** Dados, informação e conhecimento
**Duração recomendada:** 0 min
