## Contexto e Objetivo

This article explains how Big Data can enhance customer experience (CX) by enabling personalization, reducing friction, understanding behavior, targeting the right customers, predicting trends, and building loyalty. It highlights the role of AI in turning large, complex datasets into actionable insights for real-time, user-centered interactions.
Why it matters for your Module:
 * Helps design dashboards and visualizations that are user-focused.
 * Guides decisions on which data to highlight for different audiences.
 * Provides frameworks for storytelling with data and improving UX in Big Data projects.

Reflection: Consider how a digital product you use could apply these six strategies and what UX or ethical challenges might arise.
