## Contexto e Objetivo

This delves into the ethical considerations of data visualization, highlighting how design choices can influence the interpretation of data. It emphasizes that data visualizations are not neutral; they are shaped by the designer's decisions and can be used to persuade or mislead audiences. The piece discusses a project where two visualizations were created from the same dataset on abortion access in the United States, each presenting a different narrative through design choices. This exercise illustrates how data can be manipulated to support various arguments, underscoring the importance of ethical responsibility in data representation.
