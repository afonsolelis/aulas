## Contexto e Objetivo

What is Team Building for? Where to start? What aspects should I be aware of when leading a team? In this material, you will find a summary of the book Five Dysfunctions of a Team by Patrick Lencioni, which is one of the main theoretical references in the leadership process of teams. Read this material thinking about the dysfunctions you may be experiencing in your project group.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Liderança
**Assuntos:** Priorização de tarefas a partir do design
,Uso de ferramentas de colaboração
**Duração recomendada:** 0 min
