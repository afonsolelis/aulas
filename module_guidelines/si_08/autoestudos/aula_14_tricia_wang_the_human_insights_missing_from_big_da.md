## Contexto e Objetivo

In this talk, Tricia Wang challenges the idea that more data always means better insights. She shows how companies can drown in numbers but still miss the human stories that truly matter. Wang introduces the concept of “thick data”—the rich, qualitative understanding of people’s lives that complements the scale of big data.
For you as a student in this Module, this video is an eye-opener: it connects directly to the challenge of designing visualizations and user experiences that are not only data-driven but also human-centered. It demonstrates why good UX in big data projects requires going beyond dashboards and charts to actually capture meaning, empathy, and context.
