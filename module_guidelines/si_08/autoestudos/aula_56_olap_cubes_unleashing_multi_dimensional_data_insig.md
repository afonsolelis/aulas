## Contexto e Objetivo

OLAP Cubes. In this session, we will unravel the mysteries of OLAP (Online Analytical Processing) Cubes and how they revolutionize data analysis through multi-dimensional insights. We begin by breaking down the concept of OLAP Cubes and explaining how they enable users to explore data from various dimensions, providing a deeper understanding of business performance, trends, and patterns. Next, we highlight the power of multi-dimensional analysis, showing how OLAP Cubes facilitate dynamic, interactive, and intuitive data exploration. This technology allows decision-makers to slice, dice, and drill down into data with ease.
