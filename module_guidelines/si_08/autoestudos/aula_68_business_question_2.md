## Contexto e Objetivo

Argument – in favor of online
Prioritizing the online experience in omnichannel strategies is essential to ensure convenience and agility for the modern consumer, who seeks quick and accessible solutions anywhere. The exponential growth of e-commerce and the massive adoption of mobile devices demonstrate that the customer journey is increasingly digital. In addition, the online environment enables large-scale personalization, based on browsing data and purchase history—something difficult to replicate in physical spaces. Another important factor is the reduction of operational costs, since maintaining digital platforms is generally more efficient than managing physical stores.
