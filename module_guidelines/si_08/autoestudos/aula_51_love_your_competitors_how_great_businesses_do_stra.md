## Contexto e Objetivo

The author emphasizes that companies often view competitors as threats and focus on defeating them, which can foster a warlike mindset and lead to actions that harm the market. However, he argues that an effective strategy involves seeing competitors as sources of learning and potential collaboration.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Negócios
**Assuntos:** Sustentabilidade e ESG
**Duração recomendada:** 0 min
