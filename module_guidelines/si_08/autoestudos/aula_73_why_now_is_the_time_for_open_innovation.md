## Contexto e Objetivo

The article highlights the importance of open innovation in times of rapid and uncertain change, such as those we are currently experiencing. It explains that open innovation allows companies to benefit from collective intelligence and the diversity of ideas and experiences, while at the same time reducing the costs and risks associated with internal innovation.
