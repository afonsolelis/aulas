## Contexto e Objetivo

The article highlights four key pillars for a successful digital transformation: leadership, culture, technology, and talent. Leadership must define the vision and strategy, culture should create an environment of innovation, technology needs to be advanced and integrated, and talent must be recruited and trained in digital skills.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Negócios
**Assuntos:** Sustentabilidade e ESG
