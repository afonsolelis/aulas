## Contexto e Objetivo

This emphasizes the critical role of ethical considerations in creating data visualizations. It highlights how visuals, even when based on accurate data, can mislead if not handled responsibly. The piece explores various stages of the data visualization process—from data collection and analysis to presentation—and discusses how decisions made at each step can influence the clarity and integrity of the final output. Through insights from visual journalist Xaquín G. V., the article underscores the importance of transparency, context, and care in ensuring that data visualizations serve to inform rather than deceive.
