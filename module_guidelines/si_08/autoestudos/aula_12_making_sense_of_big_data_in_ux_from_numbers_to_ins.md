## Contexto e Objetivo

This article emphasizes that big data in UX isn't just about numbers; it's about deeply understanding users. Every digital interaction leaves behind valuable insights into user behaviors, preferences, and pain points. By analyzing these "digital breadcrumbs," UX designers can make informed decisions that enhance user experiences.
Why it matters for your Module:
 * Connects Data to UX Decisions: Shows how analyzing large datasets helps designers make evidence-based decisions rather than relying on assumptions.
 * Supports Your Project Goals: In your Big Data + UX Visualization project, understanding user behavior is key to designing dashboards, interfaces, and visualizations that truly meet user needs.
 * Prepares for Storytelling: Helps you identify which data points are most meaningful,…
