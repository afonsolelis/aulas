## Contexto e Objetivo

The article "Ethics of Data Visualization: Avoiding Deceptive Practices" emphasizes the importance of creating truthful, transparent, and responsible visual representations of data. It highlights the ethical responsibilities of data scientists and designers in ensuring that visualizations accurately represent data, avoid misleading interpretations, and consider the potential impact on individuals and society. The article discusses key ethical considerations such as privacy, bias, context, and accessibility, and underscores the need for professionals to be aware of the ethical dimensions of their work in data visualization.
