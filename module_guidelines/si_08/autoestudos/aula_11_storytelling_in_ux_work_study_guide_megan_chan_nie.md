## Contexto e Objetivo

This optional study presents a guide from Nielsen Norman Group that provides a comprehensive overview of how storytelling can enhance user experience (UX) work. It emphasizes the importance of crafting compelling narratives to communicate research findings and design ideas effectively. The guide is structured into several key sections:
 * Storytelling Fundamentals: Introduces the basics of storytelling in UX, including how to leverage narratives to communicate user needs and gain buy-in for UX initiatives.
 * Turning Data into Stories: Discusses methods for transforming complex data into compelling stories, helping audiences understand and engage with the information presented.
