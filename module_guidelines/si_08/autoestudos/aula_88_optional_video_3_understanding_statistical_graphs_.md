## Contexto e Objetivo

"Understanding Statistical Graphs and When to Use Them," by Dr. Nic's Maths and Stats. This self-study course explains the use and interpretation of seven different types of statistical graphs. These include the pictograph, bar chart, pie chart, dot plot, stem and leaf plot, scatterplot, and time series.

**Eixo disciplinar:** Matemática e Física
