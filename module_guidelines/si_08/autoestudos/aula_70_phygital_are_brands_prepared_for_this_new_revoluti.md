## Contexto e Objetivo

The MIT Technology Review Brazil article, “Phygital: Are Brands Prepared for This New Revolution?”, addresses the emerging concept of “phygital”, which combines physical and digital experiences to create innovative and engaging solutions for consumers. The text discusses how companies must adapt their business models and strategies to keep up with this trend and remain competitive in the market.
