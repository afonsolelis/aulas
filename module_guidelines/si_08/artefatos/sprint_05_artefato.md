# Sprint 5 — DataAPP - Final Version, Final Documentation, Final Pitch

## Descrição

Este artefato consolida as entregas da Sprint 5. As atividades abaixo compõem a avaliação da sprint.

**Bloco:** Bloco 3: Onde (Engineering + Technology)
**Visão RM-ODP:** Engineering + Technology

## Artefatos da Sprint

### DataAPP - Final Version

**Peso:** 3 | **Semana:** 10

**Objetivo:**

In this final sprint, students must refine and expand the first version of the DataAPP based on the feedback received in Sprint 4. The objective is to deliver a polished, intuitive, and fully functional analytical application that includes the approved infographic from the UX artifact of the previous sprint. The DataAPP must provide clear data presentation, responsive interactions, consistent filtering behavior, and reliable connections to the Data Cube. Improvements should incorporate usability enhancements, additional features, error handling, and performance adjustments.
Students must revise the application interface, integrate the validated infographic layout, implement improvements requested by feedback, perform thorough testing, and prepare final documentation along with a short…

**Barema:**

1. Integration of the approved infographic and incorporation of feedback.
2. Functional enhancements that improve the DataAPP’s clarity, usability, and stability.
3. Accurate and reliable connection to the Data Cube and update mechanisms.
4. Clear, complete, and well-structured documentation and final presentation.

### Final Pitch

**Peso:** 2 | **Semana:** 10

**Objetivo:**

The Final Presentation is an executive showcase of the project and the final MVP solution. Students must clearly and confidently present their work, focusing on the solution and providing a comprehensive review of the project journey. The presentation should communicate the value and impact of the solution, demonstrating clarity, professionalism, and a deep understanding of the development process.

**Barema:**

* Clarity and confidence in the presentation
 * Focus on the proposed solution and its benefits
 * Comprehensive review of the project journey and key milestones

- os requisitos não funcionais cobrem os 8 eixos da ISO/IEC 25010 com métricas verificáveis
- a matriz de rastreabilidade (RTM) está atualizada sem lacunas nos fluxos priorizados
