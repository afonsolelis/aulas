## Artefatos da Sprint

### Automated Data Cube

**Peso:** 8 | **Semana:** 6

**Objetivo:**

In this artifact, students must design, implement, and document a data cube integrated into the project’s Data Warehouse. The objective is to enable multidimensional analysis to support the dataApp, using either a star schema or snowflake modeling approach. The cube must be fully implemented in ClickHouse and must be constructed entirely from views derived from the raw JSON ingestion table. All fact and dimension views must correctly parse and CAST JSON fields into their appropriate data types, ensuring consistency, reliability, and analytical usability.
Checklist of Required Deliverables
1. Dimensional Modeling (Star Schema or Snowflake)
- Definition of the fact table structure, including granularity, metrics, and keys.
- Definition of all dimension structures, with complete attribute…

**Barema:**

1. Relevant and appropriate identification of dimensions for the dataApp.
2. Correct and consistent implementation of fact and dimension views using JSON parsing and CAST.
3. Coherent dimensional modeling aligned with star schema or snowflake.
4. Clear, detailed, and explanatory documentation of the entire process.
