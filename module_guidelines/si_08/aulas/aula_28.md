The objectives of this lesson are for students to understand the fundamental concepts of metrics and telemetry in the context of Prometheus, to learn how to configure and collect standard and custom metrics, to analyze and interpret telemetry data in real time using Prometheus, and to apply monitoring techniques to optimize the performance and reliability of systems.
