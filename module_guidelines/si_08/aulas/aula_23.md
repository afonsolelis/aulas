# Aula 23 - TOOLS FOR DATAVIS AND STRATEGIC INFOGRAPHICS

## Descrição da Aula
Objective: In this session, we will explore tools for creating infographics and their role in strategic communication of data insights. The goal is to prepare students to design infographics that highlight the right story for the right audience.

Connection to Big Data Projects: Infographics are effective tools to communicate analytical findings to non-technical audiences and support strategic decision-making.
