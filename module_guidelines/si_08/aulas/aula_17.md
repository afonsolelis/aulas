# Aula 17 - HABITS AND PRACTICES OF THE PHYGITAL CONSUMER + OMNICHANNEL

## Descrição da Aula
In this session, students will explore phygital consumer behavior and the concept of omnichannel, learning about shopping habits and preferences. The focus will be on identifying demands, integrating online and offline channels, and enhancing the shopping experience, thereby strengthening customer relationships and increasing market competitiveness.
