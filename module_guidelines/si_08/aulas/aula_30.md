# Aula 30 - INCREMENTAL, RADICAL, AND DISRUPTIVE INNOVATION

## Descrição da Aula
In this session, students will explore the concepts of radical innovation and disruptive innovation, understanding their differences and their impact on the market. Through theoretical analysis, case studies, and practical activities, students will learn to identify innovation opportunities, develop strategies to implement significant changes, and create solutions that can transform industries and business models.
