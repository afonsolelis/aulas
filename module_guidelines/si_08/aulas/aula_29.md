# Aula 29 - DATA SECURITY, AUTHORIZATIONS, AND ACCESS

## Descrição da Aula
The objectives of this lesson are for students to understand the fundamental principles of the LGPD related to information security, to recognize the importance of data governance in the context of the LGPD, and to apply good practices for protecting personal data in compliance with the regulation.
