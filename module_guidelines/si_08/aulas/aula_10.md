# Aula 10 - DASHBOARDS, INTERFACES, AND PERSUASIVE VISUALIZATION

## Descrição da Aula
Objective: In this session, we will analyze how dashboards and interfaces influence interpretation, and how visualization can be designed to persuade or mislead.

Connection to Big Data Projects: Dashboards are core tools for monitoring business and social indicators. Understanding persuasion in visualization helps design responsibly and strategically.
Content:
 * UX patterns for dashboards.
 * Interface design for decision-making.
 * Persuasive visualization vs. misleading visualization.
 * Ethical boundaries in persuasive design.
