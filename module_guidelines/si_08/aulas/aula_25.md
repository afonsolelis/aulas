# Aula 25 - DAG AUTOMATION

## Descrição da Aula
The objectives of this lesson on DAG Automation are for students to understand the concept of Directed Acyclic Graphs (DAGs) in the context of workflow automation, to learn how to create and manage DAGs using Prefect, to structure tasks and flows as DAGs for optimizing ETL and Machine Learning processes, and to apply scheduling and monitoring techniques within Prefect.
The lesson begins with an introduction to DAGs in Prefect, defining their concept and emphasizing their importance in workflow orchestration, as well as explaining how DAGs are applied to data processes. A comparison is made…
