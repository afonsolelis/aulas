2. The case and the hybrid architecture

The case is the pharmacy chain of the module, with six stores (CMP-01 Campinas, STS-02 Santos, SOR-03 Sorocaba, JUN-04 Jundiaí, BAU-05 Bauru and FRA-06 Franca), grouped in the regions Coast and Interior, and forty products, part of them continuous-use medicines. Lesson 4 built an ETL that read the Bronze layer from a local MinIO and loaded a Silver layer of conformed tables into a DuckDB file. This lesson keeps the same Bronze, produced by the same generator, and changes the design of the Silver layer in two directions. The first is the shape of the data, which becomes a single enriched table with the columns data_ingestao, data_linha and data_tag. The second is where the data lives, because the Silver layer now has two copies with different purposes.

The architecture was decided by the professor before the session and is recorded by the agent in an ADR during Step 01. Each load reads the Bronze, enriches it with DuckDB and writes first a standardised lake in the MinIO, one Parquet file per tag and per day under silver/enriched/data_tag=<tag>/ingestion_date=<D>/data.parquet, together with a manifest of the run under silver/_manifests/run_<id>.json that records the SHA-256 of every file. Only then does the load fill ClickHouse, and it does so by reading the files it has just written to the lake, never the Bronze. This order is what makes the warehouse auditable, since ClickHouse never holds a row that the lake does not hold, and ClickHouse can be rebuilt from the lake at any moment.

The two copies serve two kinds of question. ClickHouse keeps a short window, up to one year, removed automatically by a TTL, and answers the tactical questions of the operation, such as which lots expire in the next three months or how much each store sold since Monday. The lake keeps every version for years or decades and is read with DuckDB for the strategic questions, for data science and for heavy manipulations such as training runs that last hours or days, which should not compete with the tactical queries for the resources of the warehouse. Section 8 explains why the decision on the length of the ClickHouse window belongs to the business.

The environment runs in two containers declared in docker-compose.yml. The MinIO uses the image coollabsio/minio:RELEASE.2025-10-15T17-29-55Z and publishes the S3 API on port 9000 and the web console on port 9001. ClickHouse uses the image clickhouse/clickhouse-server:26.8.15.10 and publishes only its HTTP interface, on port 8123, which is the one used by clickhouse-connect. The native port of ClickHouse is also 9000, and since the MinIO already occupies that port on the host, the native port is not published.

3. The Silver layer as a Data Warehouse

The term Data Warehouse was defined by William Inmon as a subject-oriented, integrated, time-variant and non-volatile collection of data in support of the decisions of management. Each of the four properties describes a guarantee that operational systems do not give, and together they explain why the Silver layer of the medallion architecture plays the role of the warehouse in this module.

Subject-oriented

The data is organised around the entities of the business, such as store, product, sale, stock position and delivery, whatever application produced them. In the enriched table, the entity is the middle segment of each tag, as in pos.sale_line.v1.

Integrated

Codes, formats and units from different sources are made consistent. The store code of the POS and the column loja of the supplier refer to the same store after the enrichment, and every date carries the same zone.

Time-variant

Every record is associated with a moment, and the history is kept. Here the moment is data_ingestao, and the lake keeps every version that arrived, including a day of 2024 recovered from a backup.

Non-volatile

Data is loaded and read, but not edited in place. A correction arrives as a new record or as a reload derived from the Bronze, which is why a rerun of the load must produce the same result.

The Silver layer combines and cleans the Bronze into a corporate view of the core entities of the business. Corporate view means that the definition of a sale line, of a stock position or of a delivery is the same for every team that reads it, so that the purchasing team and the finance team do not compute two different revenues from two different extractions. This shared definition is what makes self-service analytics possible, in which analysts query the Silver layer directly, without asking the engineering team to reconcile the sources for each question. Self-service depends on three conditions that the lab makes explicit: a written contract with the meaning of every field, stable names, and a way of filtering the data by kind and version, which is the role of data_tag.

The Silver layer is also the foundation of the Gold layer. Lesson 6 will build data cubes and data marts for specific indicators, and those structures read the Silver layer instead of the Bronze, so that every mart inherits the same cleaning and the same keys. This division corresponds to the two classic schools of the field. Inmon proposed an integrated enterprise warehouse, normalised, from which departmental data marts are derived. Ralph Kimball proposed building the warehouse as a set of dimensional marts that share conformed dimensions. In the medallion architecture, the Silver layer takes the integrating role of the Inmon warehouse and the Gold layer takes the dimensional role of the Kimball marts, and the two approaches stop being alternatives and become successive layers.

4. The enriched table

The description of the session asks for a table with three columns, and the lab keeps their names exactly as written, in Portuguese, because the contract of the Silver layer and the queries of every later lesson depend on them. The table stores one record per row, whatever the entity, and the entity and its version are declared by the tag.

The date and time of ingestion

The value of data_ingestao is derived from the Bronze, without reference to the clock of the load. The file batch_2300.json of the partition 2026-10-12 receives 2026-10-12 23:00 in America/Sao_Paulo, the resend batch_0815_resend.json of 2026-10-18 receives 08:15, and the supplier file acme_0930.csv receives 09:30. The extract of the ERP carries its own column extracted_at, and the master files of stores and products receive 00:00. The reason for this rule is reproducibility. If the load wrote the current time, two runs over the same Bronze would produce different values in every row, the files of the lake would differ from one run to the next and a reconciliation between runs would find differences that do not correspond to any change in the data. Deriving the moment from the Bronze makes reruns deterministic.

The record in JSON

The column data_linha holds the record as a JSON object. JSON accommodates structured data, such as a sale line with fixed fields, and semi-structured data, such as a record whose fields vary from one version of a source to another, in the same column. This flexibility would be a problem in a warehouse that stored the JSON as text, because every query would have to parse the text of every row. ClickHouse avoids it with the type JSON, which stores each path of the object as a separate subcolumn with its own type, so that a query on data_linha.store_code reads only that subcolumn, as it would read an ordinary column. The format therefore remains compatible with the columnar storage of the warehouse, and a new field in a new version of the source becomes a new path without any change to the table.

In the rehearsal, ClickHouse typed the paths of the sale lines as follows: city String, continuous_use Bool, line_total Float64, product_id Int64, quantity Int64, region String, sale_id String, sold_at String, source_file String, store_code String, store_id Int64, terminal_id String and unit_price Float64. The path sold_at stayed as text only because every insert disabled the inference of dates, as section 16 explains, which preserves the offset -03:00 written by the enrichment.

The flexibility of JSON has a cost that the design must pay elsewhere. The table does not enforce which fields each record has, so the schema of each kind of record moves from the DDL to the contract of the Silver layer, specs/silver_contract.md, where each tag lists its fields, its key of deduplication and its rules. The enrichment code and its tests are what enforce that contract.

The tags

The column data_tag classifies each record by source, entity and version. It serves three purposes. It versions the records, because a change in the format of a source produces a new tag while the records of the previous version remain valid. It categorises them, because the entity is part of the tag. And it filters them, because the table is ordered by (data_tag, data_ingestao), so that a query restricted to one tag reads a contiguous range of the table. The type LowCardinality(String) stores the few distinct values of the tag in a dictionary, which reduces both the size and the cost of comparing them.

Part IIEnrichment and audit

5. Enrichment techniques

Enrichment is the set of transformations that turns a record of the Bronze into a record that the business can use without consulting other sources. The description of the session names three techniques, which are normalising formats and units, aggregating multiple sources and deriving new fields according to the requirements of the partner. The lab applies the three in the same SQL statement of src/enrich.py, after the rules of Lesson 4 have removed copies and separated the invalid records.

Normalising formats and units

Normalising means writing the same kind of value in the same way, whatever source produced it. In the Bronze of the case, store BAU-05 sends sold_at as dd/mm/yyyy HH:MM:SS in local time, while the other stores send ISO 8601 with the offset -03:00; store SOR-03 sends unit_price as text with a decimal comma; about one line in ten has a store code in lower case, in title case or with spaces; and the supplier writes its headers in Portuguese, with decimal commas and dates in the Brazilian format. After the enrichment, every timestamp is ISO 8601 with the offset, every price is a number with a decimal point in reais, every store code is trimmed and in upper case, and every field has a name in English. Without this step, a filter by store_code = 'BAU-05' would miss part of the lines of that store, and the revenue of SOR-03 would be computed only over the lines whose price could be read.

Aggregating multiple sources

Aggregating, in the sense of the description, means bringing into one record the attributes that live in different sources. A sale line of the POS carries the store and the product, but not the city and region of the store nor whether the product is of continuous use, which come from the master files of the ERP. The enrichment joins the masters and writes city, region and continuous_use inside data_linha. The record becomes self-contained, so that an analyst can group the sales by region without knowing where the regions are defined, and so that the record keeps the attributes that were valid at the moment of its ingestion. Section 10 discusses the price of this choice, which is redundancy.

Deriving new fields

Deriving means computing a field that no source sends but that a requirement of the partner needs. The sale line receives line_total, the product of the quantity and the unit price. In Step 03, version 2 of the supplier file receives days_to_expiry, the number of days between the delivery and the expiry of the lot, because the tactical question of the purchasing team is which lots expire in less than ninety days. A derived field belongs to the Silver layer when the rule is stable and shared by several consumers, and it belongs to the Gold layer when it serves one indicator only.

Deduplication, quarantine and the accounting of rows

The rules of Lesson 4 remain in force. Sale lines are deduplicated by sale_id, stock positions by store, product and date keeping the latest extract, and deliveries by id_entrega. Records that break a rule go to quarantine.v1 with a reason, and every record read from the Bronze ends in exactly one tag. The generator prints the defects it planted, and the counts of the Silver layer must be explained by them.

6. Auditability: the lake first

A warehouse is auditable when every number it returns can be traced to the data that produced it and when that data can be shown to be the same data that was loaded. The hybrid design obtains this property through the order of the load, which always writes the lake, then loads ClickHouse from the files of the lake, and writes the manifest last.

StageWhat it doesMeasured in the rehearsal (Step 02)

The order has three consequences. The first is that ClickHouse never holds a row that the lake does not hold, because the only input of ClickHouse is the set of lake files of the run. If the load of ClickHouse fails, the manifest is written with the status error_clickhouse and the process exits with a non-zero code, so that the scheduler knows that the tactical copy is out of date while the lake remains complete. The second is that ClickHouse is disposable, in the sense that it can be rebuilt from the lake at any moment. In the rehearsal, rebuilding it from the 63 lake files of Step 03 sent 26,453 rows in 6.88 s. The third is that the integrity of the lake can be checked later, because the manifest keeps the SHA-256 of every file. The reconciliation of Step 03 recomputes the hash of each file listed in the last manifest, and any difference means that a file was changed after the load.

A rerun over the same Bronze must arrive at the same result, and the design ensures it in two ways. Since data_ingestao comes from the Bronze, the content of every record is also the same in both runs.

Part IIIWarehouses and lifecycle

7. Columnar warehouses: Redshift and ClickHouse

Operational databases store each row contiguously, because a transaction usually reads or writes a whole record, such as one sale. Analytical queries behave differently, since they read a few columns of millions of rows, such as the price and the store of every sale of a month. A columnar warehouse stores each column contiguously, so that such a query reads only the columns it needs. Values of the same column are similar to one another, which makes compression much more effective, and the engine can process blocks of values of one column at a time, which is called vectorised execution. Amazon Redshift and ClickHouse, the two tools named in the description of the session, are columnar warehouses, and so is DuckDB, used in the lab as the engine of the lake.

ClickHouse and the MergeTree engine

ClickHouse is an open-source columnar database for analytical workloads, which can run in a container, as in the lab, on a cluster or as a managed cloud service. Its main family of table engines is MergeTree. Each insert writes an immutable part, with the rows sorted by the key of ORDER BY, and a background process merges small parts into larger ones. The table has a sparse primary index, with one entry for each granule of rows (8,192 rows by default), so that a filter on the first columns of the sorting key skips the granules that cannot contain matching rows. In the lab, the sorting key (data_tag, data_ingestao) makes a filter by tag and period read only the granules of that tag in that period.

Two other clauses of the DDL organise the data in time. PARTITION BY toYYYYMM(data_ingestao) keeps the parts of each month separate, which allows the engine to drop or skip a whole month at once. TTL toDateTime(data_ingestao) + INTERVAL 1 YEAR declares that a row expires one year after its ingestion, and ClickHouse removes expired rows during merges and, as the rehearsal showed, already at insert time. After Step 02 the table had 5 parts and 623.9 KiB compressed for 24,677 rows.

Amazon Redshift

Amazon Redshift is the managed warehouse of AWS, offered as provisioned clusters or as Redshift Serverless. It is a massively parallel processing (MPP) system, in which a leader node plans each query and compute nodes execute it in parallel over slices of the data, stored in columns. Two choices of the table design decide its performance. The distribution style (KEY, ALL, EVEN or AUTO) decides on which node each row lives, and a good choice puts the rows that are joined together on the same node, avoiding traffic between nodes. The sort key decides the order of the rows on disk, and Redshift keeps the minimum and maximum of each block (zone maps) to skip blocks, playing a role similar to the sorting key of MergeTree. Redshift Spectrum reads files in S3 through external tables, so that a query can combine tables of the warehouse with Parquet files of a lake, and the type SUPER stores semi-structured values queried with PartiQL, which is the counterpart of the JSON type of ClickHouse.

8. Lifecycle: tactical and strategic windows

Data has a lifecycle, and the questions asked about it change with its age. Recent data is read often, by many people, with short questions about the operation, and it must answer in a fraction of a second. Old data is read rarely, by few people, with long questions about trends, and it can take seconds or minutes. The hybrid design gives each phase a place. ClickHouse keeps the tactical window, and the lake with DuckDB keeps the strategic history.

Its place is the lake for another reason, which is that ClickHouse no longer holds the rows of 2024 and cannot answer it. The same reasoning applies to data science, whose training runs read years of data for hours or days and would degrade the tactical queries if they ran on the warehouse.

The window is a business decision

The length of the ClickHouse window, one year in the lab, is a business decision, not a technical one. Technically, ClickHouse could keep a month or a decade, and changing the window is a single statement, ALTER TABLE silver.enriched MODIFY TTL .... What the window should be depends on questions that only the business can answer: how far back the tactical users look in their daily work, how much the partner is willing to pay to keep data in the fast copy, and which legal obligations or limits apply, such as fiscal retention periods and the principle of minimisation of the LGPD for personal data. The owner of the decision is the business area of the partner that uses the tactical queries, together with whoever answers for data governance. For this reason the contract and the ADR of the lab mark the one-year retention as a hypothesis to validate with the partner, and the engineering team implements whatever value the business decides.

Decision for the group

In the artifact Data Ingestion Structure, the group should state the retention of its warehouse as a hypothesis, name who in the partner organisation decides it, and record the question that will validate it, for example how many months back the users of the DataApp compare their indicators.

9. Schema evolution

Sources change their formats, and a warehouse that requires a change of its tables for every change of a source becomes a bottleneck of the project. From 2026-10-26, the supplier ACME sends a version 2 of its daily file, acme_v2_0930.csv, with two more columns, lote (the lot number) and validade (the expiry date). Everything else stays as in version 1. In the first day, the file brings 18 deliveries, 6 of them with less than 90 days to expiry.

In a warehouse of one table per entity, the new columns would require an ALTER TABLE on the delivery table, a decision about the value of the new columns in the old rows and a coordinated change in every query that lists the columns. In the enriched table, version 2 becomes a new tag, supplier.delivery.v2, whose data_linha carries lot, expires_on and the derived days_to_expiry, and the DDL of ClickHouse does not change, because the type JSON adds the new paths when they first appear. The consumers of version 1 are not affected, and a consumer that needs both versions filters by data_tag IN ('supplier.delivery.v1', 'supplier.delivery.v2').

The version in the tag should change when a record stops meaning the same thing for its consumers, such as when a field changes type or meaning or when the source sends a new format. A field added to a format that is otherwise unchanged can remain in the same version, as long as the contract registers it. The enrichment must also keep reading version 1 when no file of version 2 exists, which was the source of the most instructive error of the rehearsal, described in section 16.

10. Third Normal Form

The enriched table records what arrived, with each record self-contained, but analysts usually need a model with one table per entity and explicit relationships among them. The first model presented in the session is the relational model in Third Normal Form, defined by Edgar F. Codd, who proposed the relational model in 1970 and the second and third normal forms in 1971. Normalisation organises the tables so that each fact is stored in one place only, which prevents the anomalies that redundancy causes when the data changes.

The tool of normalisation is the functional dependency. An attribute B depends functionally on an attribute A when each value of A determines a single value of B. In the case, store_id determines city and region, and product_id determines the name of the product and whether it is of continuous use. When a table stores an attribute that depends on something other than its key, the same fact is repeated in many rows, and three anomalies appear. Suppose that each sale line stored the city and the region of its store. Reassigning a store to another region would require updating thousands of sale lines, and forgetting one would leave the data contradictory, which is the update anomaly. A new store could not be recorded before its first sale, which is the insertion anomaly. And deleting the last sale of a store would erase the only record of its city, which is the deletion anomaly.

Normal formRequirementExample in the case

2NF1NF, and no attribute depends on only part of a composite keyin a stock position keyed by (store_id, product_id, date), the name of the product depends only on product_id and moves to product
3NF2NF, and no attribute depends on the key through another non-key attribute (transitive dependency)in sale_line, city depends on store_id, which depends on sale_id; city and region live only in store

Step 04 builds the model in Third Normal Form as DuckDB views over the lake files, with the tables store, product, supplier, sale_line, delivery and lot. In the rehearsal the views returned store 6, product 40, supplier 1, sale_line 22,385, delivery 292 and lot 18, with 0 sale lines without a product. The strength of the model is integrity, because each fact has one place and the relationships are explicit, which is why Inmon recommended it for the integrated enterprise warehouse. Its limitation in a warehouse is history. A table in Third Normal Form represents the current state of each entity, and keeping the previous values, such as the region of a store before a reorganisation, requires adding validity dates to the tables, which complicates the model and the queries. The enriched table makes the opposite choice, repeating the city and region in every sale line, and in exchange each record preserves the attributes that were valid when it arrived.

11. Data Vault

Data Vault is a modelling method for the integrated layer of a warehouse, created by Dan Linstedt in the 1990s and revised as Data Vault 2.0, described by Linstedt and Michael Olschimke in 2015. It was designed for environments in which sources are added and changed often and in which the full history must be kept for audit. The method separates three things that a table in Third Normal Form keeps together, which are the identity of an entity, its relationships and its descriptive attributes.

Hub

One row per business key, such as the code of a store or the identifier of a sale, with the hash key, the moment of the first load and the source. A hub holds identity only.

Link

One row per relationship among business keys, such as the sale with its store and its product, identified by the hash of the combined keys.

Satellite

The descriptive attributes of a hub or a link, with their history. Each change of the attributes adds a new row with a new load_ts.

Every table of the vault carries the same technical columns. The hash key is a hash of the business key, md5 in the lab, which allows hubs, links and satellites to be loaded in parallel, since a link can compute the key of a hub from the business key without looking it up. The column load_ts records when the row was loaded, and record_source records where it came from. Satellites usually also carry a hash_diff, a hash of all the descriptive attributes, which the load compares with the latest row of the same key to decide whether anything changed. The vault is insert-only, so that nothing is updated or deleted and the state at any past moment can be reconstructed from the rows loaded until then.

The adaptability of the method comes from this separation. A new source of an existing entity adds a satellite, a new relationship adds a link, and the existing tables and the loads that fill them remain untouched. In Step 04, version 2 of the supplier required only a new satellite, sat_delivery_lot, while hubs and links did not change. In the rehearsal, the views returned hub_store 6, hub_product 40, hub_sale 22,385, link_sale 22,385, sat_sale 22,385, hub_delivery 292, sat_delivery 292 and sat_delivery_lot 18.

SQL · satellite of the lots (example of prompt 04)

The price of the method is the number of tables and joins. A question as simple as the revenue by region joins a link, two hubs and their satellites, so analysts seldom query the raw vault, and a presentation layer, such as the marts of the Gold layer, is built on top of it. In the lab the vault is made of views over the lake, which shows its structure without creating a second copy of the data. In production the vault is usually materialised, because the views would recompute the hashes at every query.

16. Recurring mistakes of agents

The reference implementation was built with the same prompts on 02/11/2026, and the mistakes below appeared in that rehearsal. Most of them pass a superficial test and are caught only by a count or by the type of a column, which is why the prompts of the lab already carry the corresponding adjustments.

The first mistake concerns time zones. DuckDB returns values of type TIMESTAMPTZ to Python only when the package pytz is installed, and without it the load fails at the moment of converting data_ingestao. The package is in requirements.txt for this reason. A related mistake happens in ClickHouse. The JSON type infers dates and timestamps from strings by default, so sold_at, written by the enrichment as 2026-10-12T20:25:08-03:00, became a timestamp without the offset. Every insert must therefore use input_format_try_infer_dates = 0 and input_format_try_infer_datetimes = 0, and the review of Step 02 checks that sold_at stayed String.

The second mistake concerns the evolution of the supplier file. Reading the CSV files of both versions with union_by_name works while a file of version 2 exists, but when none exists yet the query fails with a Binder Error saying that the relation does not have a column named lote. The enrichment must add the missing columns explicitly, so that version 1 keeps loading alone, which is the second question of the review of Step 03.

The third group of mistakes concerns the connection to the services. ClickHouse 26.8 enables async_insert by default, which makes an insert return before the rows are visible, so a count right after the load can return fewer rows than were sent. Setting async_insert = 0 makes each insert synchronous. The MinIO and the native port of ClickHouse both use port 9000, so only the HTTP port 8123 of ClickHouse is published, and an agent that tries the native protocol finds the MinIO instead. DuckDB reaches the MinIO only with URL_STYLE 'path' and USE_SSL false, and the secret must be created at the start of each session with CREATE OR REPLACE SECRET, never with CREATE PERSISTENT SECRET, which would store the password in the profile of the user.

The fourth mistake comes from the version of DuckDB. In DuckDB 2.0 the relations returned by con.sql(...) no longer have the method fetchone(), which agents trained on older code still write, and fetchall() must be used instead.

The last mistake concerns the reconciliation. Since the TTL removes expired rows already at insert time, a reconciliation that compares ClickHouse with the whole lake reports a difference of 1,758 rows after Step 03, although both copies are correct. The comparison must be made with the rows of the lake inside the window, and the window must be read from the TTL of the table.

18. Glossary

Columnar storage
Storage in which the values of each column are kept together, so that analytical queries read only the columns they use.
data_ingestao
Column of the enriched table with the moment at which the record entered the platform, derived from the partition and the file name of the Bronze.
data_linha
Column of the enriched table with the normalised and derived fields of one record, in JSON.
data_tag
Column of the enriched table with the source, the entity and the version of the record, or quarantine.v1.
Data Vault
Modelling method that separates identity (hubs), relationships (links) and descriptive attributes with history (satellites), loaded insert-only.
Functional dependency
Relation in which each value of one attribute determines a single value of another.
hash_diff
Hash of the descriptive attributes of a satellite row, compared with the latest row of the same key to detect a change.
Hash key
Hash of a business key used as the key of hubs and links in a Data Vault, which allows parallel loads.
Manifest
JSON file written at the end of each load with the keys, rows, bytes and SHA-256 of every lake file, the rows per tag, the times and the status.
MergeTree
Family of table engines of ClickHouse that writes immutable sorted parts and merges them in the background.
MPP
Massively parallel processing: execution of a query in parallel across nodes that each hold a slice of the data, as in Redshift.
Reconciliation
Comparison of the counts of the lake inside the window with the counts of ClickHouse, per tag, plus the check of the SHA-256 of the lake files.
Schema evolution
Change in the format of a source over time, absorbed in the enriched table by a new tag.
Self-service analytics
Analysis done directly by business users over documented data, without a request to the engineering team for each question.
Third Normal Form
Normal form in which no non-key attribute depends on the key through another non-key attribute.
TTL
Time to live: rule of a ClickHouse table that removes rows after a declared interval.

  • The three columns are named exactly data_ingestao, data_linha and data_tag.

  • No field customer_cpf in any tag, quarantine included.
