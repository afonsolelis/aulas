Lesson plan · Data Warehouses: the hybrid Silver layer

Session in which the professor builds live, with a coding agent and four CREATE prompts over the raw CSV files of Olist, a Silver layer kept in two copies, a long-lived lake read with DuckDB and ClickHouse with a one-year retention, reconciles both copies and models over them a star schema with views and materialized views that compares the monthly sales of the North and the Southeast.

About this session

Data Warehouses: the hybrid Silver layer · 05/11/2026 · Prof. Afonso

Learning objective

By the end of the session, students should be able to design the Silver layer of a Data Warehouse for the Olist orders as an enriched table with the data_ingestao, data_linha and data_tag columns, loaded first into a long-lived lake read with DuckDB and then into ClickHouse with a one-year retention, to reconcile both copies, and to build over it a star schema with views and materialized views that answers how many sales the North made each month compared with the Southeast, justifying the grain, the dimensions and the retention.

Session strategy

Practice and theory interleaved. The professor builds the Silver layer and the star schema live with a coding agent in four steps (contract and decision, hybrid load, audit and lifecycle, and star schema), pasting each CREATE prompt over the raw CSV files of Olist, and while the agent works the slides explain the concept that the step implements. Each step ends with a review against counts measured in the rehearsal, and the session closes with the connection to the Data Ingestion Structure artifact.

Session structure

  1. Daily — 15 min
  2. Running case, hybrid architecture, raw data and local environment — 10 min
  3. Step 01: contract of the enriched table and ADR; the Silver layer as a Data Warehouse and the data_ingestao, data_linha and data_tag columns — 15 min
  4. Step 02: enrichment and hybrid load; normalising, aggregating and deriving, and auditability with the lake first — 20 min
  5. Step 03: audit and lifecycle; Redshift and ClickHouse, TTL and the clock of the retention — 15 min
  6. Step 04: star schema over the Silver layer; dimensional modelling, views and materialized views, and the North against the Southeast — 27 min
  7. Verification and the Data Ingestion Structure artifact — 10 min
  8. Closing and self-study — 8 min

2. Learning objective

By the end of the session, students should be able to design the Silver layer of a Data Warehouse for the Olist orders as an enriched table with the data_ingestao, data_linha and data_tag columns, loaded first into a long-lived lake read with DuckDB and then into ClickHouse with a one-year retention, to reconcile both copies, and to build over it a star schema with views and materialized views that answers how many sales the North made each month compared with the Southeast, justifying the grain, the dimensions and the retention.

Observable outcomes

  • Relate the Silver layer to the four properties by which Inmon defined a Data Warehouse (subject-oriented, integrated, time-variant and non-volatile).
  • Describe the enriched table with the columns data_ingestao, data_linha and data_tag, and explain how JSON and versioned tags absorb records of different shapes without a change of DDL.
  • Apply the three operations of enrichment (normalising, aggregating sources and deriving fields) to the defects measured in the Bronze of Olist, with rejected records kept under quarantine.v1 and a reason.
  • Explain why every load writes the lake first, then ClickHouse from the lake, then the manifest, and what the SHA-256 and the rebuild prove about origin, integrity and reproducibility.
  • Compare Amazon Redshift and ClickHouse as columnar warehouses, and read the partition key, the ordering key and the TTL of a MergeTree table.
  • Argue that the retention window is a business decision with an owner, and that the clock it counts from (ingestion date or purchase date) changes what the tactical copy keeps.
  • Design a star schema in the four decisions of Kimball (process, grain, dimensions and facts), with dimensions as views and the fact and the aggregate as refreshable materialized views.
  • Count a sale as a distinct order and check the answer of the business question in three sources: the aggregate, the fact with its dimensions and DuckDB over the lake.
  • Choose between a normal view and a materialized view by comparing freshness against the time and the rows read.

3. Session products

Contract and decision record

The contract specs/silver_contract.md with the three columns, the tag catalogue and the layout of the lake, the ADR docs/adr/ADR-002-hybrid-silver.md with the one-year retention written as a hypothesis with an owner, and the DDL of silver.enriched applied in ClickHouse.

Hybrid Silver layer, reconciled

347,578 rows in six tags, 16 of them in quarantine, written to six Parquet files of the lake with a manifest and loaded into ClickHouse from those files; the reconciliation reports OK for every tag with no SHA-256 mismatch, and the rebuild from the lake reaches the same counts.

Star schema and the answer

The database gold with four dimensions as views, the fact fact_order_item and the aggregate agg_sales_month_region as refreshable materialized views, and the answer North 1,796 against Southeast 66,193 delivered orders, identical in the three sources.

Continuity

The lab is a worked example of the artifact Data Ingestion Structure, weight 6 on Adalove, which each group writes for the partner data during the week. Lesson 6, on 10/11, builds the cubes and data marts of the Gold layer over this Silver layer.

7. Methodology

The professor runs the whole lab alone on the classroom computer while the class follows on the projector, so that the forty minutes of setup that four groups would need are spent on the concepts instead. Each step starts with a CREATE prompt pasted into the coding agent, and the time in which the agent writes is used to explain the concept that the step implements; the review that closes the step compares the result with the counts of the rehearsal, which are deterministic because the input is a fixed archive of real data. The defects of the source were not planted, which makes the treatment of each one a rule of the contract that the class can relate to a count.

The participation of the groups happens on the class copy of ClickHouse, where the four checks of Step 02 and the nine slides of the groups' turn in Step 04 let each group read the Silver layer and build a view and a materialized view of its own. The comparison between the two kinds of view, with the time and the rows read shown by Play, turns the choice between freshness and speed into an observation instead of a definition. The verification and the slide of the artifact then show that the lab is a worked example of the Data Ingestion Structure, which the groups repeat with the partner data during the week.

8. Assessment of learning

Evidence from the groups

The session has no graded activity in class. Within the session, the evidence of learning is the report of each group on shrek2 (slide 62): the group chooses one question of the business among late deliveries by region, freight by category and sellers far from customers, builds its own dimension, fact and report in the database lab, once as a view and once as a materialized view, and delivers the statements in the order they were run, the result of the chart query and one sentence on why the report was kept as a view or as a materialized view.

The summative evaluation of the content takes place in the artifact Data Ingestion Structure, delivered as Data_Ingestion.md with weight 6 on Adalove and following the Sprint Increment Delivery Checklist of the module. Its criteria, as presented on slide 64, are:

  • Efficient and appropriate extraction from Object Storage, adapted to the formats and partitions of the partner data.
  • Transformations that ensure quality, with the rules for the defects measured in the partner data and the accounting of rows.
  • Correct loading into the OLAP database, with the evidence that the counts of the copies agree and the window agreed with the partner.
  • A clear UML component diagram from the collection of the data to the DataApp.
  • Detailed documentation: the contract of the Silver layer, the ADR with the retention as a hypothesis and the decisions of the group.

10. Risks and contingencies

Counts different from the rehearsal

The counts are deterministic, so a difference points to a broken rule. Section 17 of the material lists the traps met in the rehearsal, such as zip prefixes read as integers, the byte order mark of the translation file and timestamps converted with a fixed offset.

Aggregate empty or out of date

A manual refresh of the fact does not refresh the aggregate, because DEPENDS ON orders only the scheduled refreshes. After a load, the fact is refreshed and awaited first and the aggregate next, and if the aggregate returns 0 it is refreshed again (slide 46).

Step 04 exceeding its time

Step 04 holds the theory of the star, the answer, the fourteen statements of the Gold database and the nine slides of the groups' turn. The support material, the prompts and the reference numbers are in the material and in lab/lesson-5/, so what does not fit is repeated by the groups during the week.

Groups on the same shared database

All groups create objects in the same database lab. Every statement is written with the prefix of the group, and objects of other groups must not be dropped; the user of the class can drop objects only inside lab.

Credentials on the projector

The file .env holds the passwords of RustFS and ClickHouse. It is changed off screen, and the rules of AGENTS.md forbid the agent to read or print it, referring to the credentials only by the names of the variables.

Sales counted as items

An answer with count() over the fact counts items, 112,642 for 99,433 orders, and overstates every month. The review of Step 04 asks for the distinct count and for the same numbers in the three sources.

11. Closing

The session closes with the verification of the lab and with what each group takes to the partner data. For the artifact, each group writes the enriched table of the partner data with the three columns and a tag catalogue, the ADR of the copies with the retention, its clock and its owner as a hypothesis, one question of the partner answered by a star schema with its grain and dimensions, and the UML component diagram up to the DataApp. For Lesson 6, each group brings a reconciliation of the copies with its own counts, the questions of the partner classified as tactical or strategic and the defects found in the partner data with the tag that receives each one.
