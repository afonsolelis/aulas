What generative AI changes in business work

Earlier analytical models predicted a number from structured data, whereas generative models produce text, tables, code and images from an instruction written in natural language, which moves AI into tasks that were done by people.

Cost of a first draft

Producing content becomes cheap

A first version of a report, an email or a query costs seconds, so the scarce resource in the team shifts from producing the draft to checking whether it is correct.

Interface

Language as the way in

A commercial manager can ask a question of the sales data in plain language, as Genie allows, without waiting for an analyst to write the query, and the quality of the question starts to matter more.

From answer to action

Systems that execute steps

Recent systems do more than answer: they call tools, read files and complete tasks of several steps, which raises the question of who authorises each step and who is accountable for it.

The three changes have the same consequence for a partner: the decision about where AI enters a process is a management decision about quality, accountability and risk, and it cannot be delegated entirely to the technology team.
Three categories of AI solution

The programme uses three working categories to describe how an AI solution reaches the business, and one solution can belong to more than one of them.

Embedded

AI inside a tool already in use

The capability arrives inside software the team already opens every day, such as Copilot in Excel, Outlook and Teams. Adoption is fast because the workflow does not change, and for the same reason it can spread without anyone deciding where it is appropriate.

Vertical

AI built for one domain

The solution is designed or configured for a specific industry or function, with its data and vocabulary, such as route planning for distribution or demand forecasting by outlet and season. It solves a narrower problem and depends on the quality of the domain data.

Agentic

AI that pursues a goal in steps

The system receives a goal, decides the sequence of steps and uses tools to carry them out, for example collecting the week's sell-out, comparing it with the plan and drafting the alert to the regional manager. Autonomy brings speed and requires explicit limits.

Agent and workflow distinction: Anthropic, "Building effective agents" (2024). Examples are generic and do not describe Ambev systems.

The five levels of AI as reported in 2024

In July 2024 Bloomberg reported an internal scale that OpenAI shared with its staff to track progress towards more capable systems. It is one company's scale and not a technical standard, but it gives a vocabulary to compare what a solution does.

Capability and autonomy are separate decisions

A capable system does not have to act alone. Morris and co-authors separate what a system can do from how much autonomy it receives, and the second choice belongs to whoever deploys it.

ToolAI as a tool

The person does the task and uses AI for one sub-task, such as a formula in Excel.

ConsultantAI as a consultant

AI gives an opinion when asked, and the person decides whether to follow it.

CollaboratorAI as a collaborator

Person and AI share the task, each with its own part.

ExpertAI as an expert

AI does the task, and the person guides and reviews it.

AgentAI as an agent

AI acts on its own, within the limits it received.

The same model can be deployed as a consultant in a pricing decision and as an agent in the scheduling of deliveries. The level of autonomy should follow the cost of an error in each process, which is a judgement the partner is better placed to make than the vendor.

Morris, M. R. et al. (2024). Levels of AGI for operationalizing progress on the path to AGI. ICML 2024.

Jobs seen as bundles of tasks

Labour economists study the effect of technology on work at the level of tasks, because technology rarely replaces a whole occupation and usually changes the mix of tasks inside it.

Tasks

What the role does

A trade marketing analyst consolidates spend by outlet, checks it against contracts, writes the monthly report and discusses the results with the sales managers. Each of these tasks has a different exposure to generative AI.

Skills

What the person brings

Knowing which outlet deserves an exception, negotiating with a supermarket buyer and noticing that a number is implausible are skills that cross several tasks and that AI does not perform on the person's behalf.

When AI takes over a task, the skills used in that task do not disappear. They move to the tasks that remain, such as checking the output, explaining it to the client and deciding the exception, and the leader has to plan that move.

Autor, Levy and Murnane (2003); Eloundou et al. (2024), Science.

The uneven frontier of AI capability

Field experiments show that the effect of generative AI depends on whether the task falls inside or outside what the model does well, and the boundary between the two is not visible in advance.

Consultants, BCG

Dell'Acqua et al. (2023)

Consultants with access to GPT-4 produced better work on tasks inside the model's frontier. On a task chosen to be outside it, those who used AI reached the correct answer less often than those who worked without it, because they accepted the output.

Customer support

Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond (2025)

An AI assistant for support agents raised the number of issues resolved per hour, with the largest gains among less experienced agents, which suggests that AI spread practices of the best agents to the rest.

For a leader, the practical result of both studies is that the same tool can help one task and harm another in the same role, so the decision about where to use it has to be made task by task and revisited as the tools change.
Three career trajectories: displaced, augmented, builder

The programme uses three categories to describe what happens to a role when AI enters it, and in most teams the three coexist inside the same area.

Displaced

Most tasks are automated

The role consists largely of tasks that AI performs at lower cost, such as keying orders or consolidating standard reports. The leader's responsibility is to anticipate the change and plan the transition of the people, instead of discovering it after the tool is installed.

Augmented

AI raises the output of the role

The core of the role remains, and AI takes over parts of it, such as the first draft of a promotion analysis. Productivity rises if the person learns to verify the output and uses the time freed for judgement and relationship.

Builder

The role designs AI for others

The person configures assistants, agents and data spaces for the team, often without writing code, using platforms such as Copilot Studio or a Genie space. This role needs domain knowledge more than technical training.

On automation and augmentation as one design choice: Raisch and Krakowski (2021); Daugherty and Wilson (2018).

What changes in the leader's role

Studies of organisations that compete on analytics and of companies that scaled AI converge on the finding that the obstacles which most often decide the outcome lie in management practices, such as decision ownership and the redesign of work.

Decisions

Make the decision explicit

A data or AI initiative starts from a recurring decision with a known owner and a known frequency. Without it, the team produces dashboards and models that nobody uses, as happened with the Maré Alta churn model, which ran for months outside any commercial routine.

Questions

Ask before funding

The leader does not need to build the analysis, but needs to ask which comparison supports a claim, which metric was used to judge a model and which premise an estimate depends on.

Work design

Redesign how the team works

Fountaine, McCarthy and Saleh found that companies which scaled AI reorganised work across functions, and treated the tool as one part of a change in the process.

Accountability

Keep the decision with people

An output produced by AI enters a decision only after someone accountable has checked it, and the leader defines who that person is in each process.

Davenport and Harris (2007); Fountaine, McCarthy and Saleh (2019), Harvard Business Review.

A Lean lens on leading AI adoption

Lean management offers practices that transfer directly to AI adoption, because both are about improving a process through the people who run it every day.

Go and see

Start where the work happens

Before choosing a tool, observe the decision in its real routine: who prepares the numbers, how long it takes and where the information arrives late. The use case comes from that observation.

Standard work

Write down how outputs are checked

A checking routine for AI outputs, written and shared, lets the team improve it over time and prevents each person from trusting the tool to a different degree.

Respect for people

Make errors safe to report

People report a wrong AI output only if reporting it carries no penalty. Edmondson calls this condition psychological safety and links it to learning in teams.

Liker (2004), The Toyota Way; Edmondson (1999), Administrative Science Quarterly.

About this session

Module 2 — Leadership in the Age of Data and AI · 07/12/2026 · Prof. Kim

Learning objective

By the end of the session, participants should be able to place an AI solution in a category (embedded, vertical, agentic) and in a level of capability and autonomy, break a role of their team into tasks to judge where AI automates, augments or leaves the work unchanged, describe what a leader has to ask, decide and protect when AI enters the routine of the area, and record in the Caderno do Sócio the recurring decisions of their area and at least one decision in which data or AI would change the outcome.

Session strategy

Strategic afternoon of four hours in which the Maré Alta case does not advance and serves only as a shared example. Three blocks combine short exposition with practices in pairs and at the tables: mapping the AI uses already present in each area by category and level of autonomy, breaking a real role into tasks, and discussing what the team needs from its leader through a Lean lens. The last hour is protected in full for the Day 1 section of the Caderno do Sócio, written individually with English prompts and timers, followed by a pair-sharing round, two readings aloud and the hand-off to Day 2.
