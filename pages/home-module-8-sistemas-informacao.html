4
ETL: Object Storage to Data Warehouse

📅 29/10/2026

An ETL pipeline built in four rounds with a coding agent: the Bronze layer of a local MinIO in JSON, Parquet and CSV is extracted with DuckDB, cleaned, formatted, converted and enriched, and loaded into a DuckDB warehouse as the Silver layer in full and incremental modes.

🎞️ Slides 📝 Material
Week 4 02/11 – 08/11/2026
5
Data Warehouses: the hybrid Silver layer

📅 05/11/2026

The Silver layer of the Olist orders as a hybrid Data Warehouse, built live with a coding agent from the raw CSV files: an enriched table with the data_ingestao, data_linha and data_tag columns, written first to a long-lived lake read with DuckDB and then to ClickHouse with a one-year retention, reconciled, and modelled as a star schema with views and materialized views that compares the monthly sales of the North and the Southeast.
